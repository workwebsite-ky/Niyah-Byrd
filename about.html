About the studio

Meet Niyah.

The hands, the parts and the point of view behind Hair by niyahhleyy.

The founder

Niyah Byrd

Hair by niyahhleyy started the way most good things do — with a passion for making people feel like themselves. Niyah built her chair on the Southside of St. Petersburg around one idea: braids should be clean, comfortable, and worth every dollar.

That means straight parts, tension that protects your edges, and pricing that already includes the hair — so booking is simple and there are no surprises when you sit down. Whether it's a full head of knotless, sharp stitch cornrows, or soft locs, the goal is the same: a style that looks fresh the day of and holds up for weeks after.
