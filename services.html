Services

Every style, one clean chair.

From protective knotless installs to crisp stitch cornrows and soft locs — each service below comes with the braiding hair included. Prices start where noted; see the full breakdown on the pricing page.

01 — Protective

Knotless Braids

A feed-in start means no bulky knot at the root — so your braids sit flat, move naturally, and stay easy on your edges. Choose your size from small to large, or a knotless bob for something shorter.

  • Sizes: small, smedium, medium, large
  • Bob option: small knotless bob
  • Good to know: small knotless is a two-day service

from $160 hair included

02 — Signature

Fulani Braids

Center-part cornrows blended with braids for that classic fulani movement. Wear it long, as a bob, or as a quick weave — including the popular jayda wayda and a simple four-braid fulani.

  • Full & bob: fulani braids and fulani bob
  • Quick weave: with braiding hair, full or bob
  • Styles: jayda wayda & 4-braid fulani

from $80 hair included

03 — Sleek

Stitch Braids & Cornrows

Clean, ridged stitch parts that read sharp from every angle. Go for a braided ponytail, a couple of feed-ins, or a full head of cornrows — anywhere from two braids to twenty rows.

  • Ponytails: small, medium, large braided ponytail
  • Cornrows: from 2 braids up to 15–20 rows
  • Finish: stitch parts and edges included

from $50 hair included

04 — Natural

Soft Locs & Natural Styles

Soft locs for a lightweight, lived-in loc look, plus natural styling that keeps your own texture healthy. Great as a protective reset between braided styles.

  • Soft locs: full install
  • Natural styles: ask in your Facebook message
  • Care: gentle on your natural hair

from $200 hair included
